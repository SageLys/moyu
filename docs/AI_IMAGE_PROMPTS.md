# AI 图片提示词

本文件记录《假装正在工作》HTML Demo 的 AI 图片资产提示词。AI 图只用于背景、开始界面、结局插画和氛围/墙面参考；点击判定、QTE 高亮、hover 描边、错误红闪、资源条和 UI 图标仍由 SVG / Canvas 负责。

统一风格：低饱和设计配色下的极简荒诞职场 Flash 卡通风，粗描边，扁平色块，强可读性，横版办公室小剧场。避免写实、3D、厚涂、高饱和霓虹、恐怖片质感、真实公司 Logo、可读文字、写实人物脸。

## Batch 1: 核心游戏背景

### office_background_base

英文提示词：

```text
A 16:9 side-view office cross-section stage background for a small Flash-style cartoon web game. Muted design colors, thick outlines, flat color blocks, slightly absurd workplace comedy tone with mild pressure. The lower center area is left clean for the main player desk overlay, the left area is left clean for a coworker desk overlay, the right side has file cabinets and office clutter, and the back layer contains a corridor and meeting room direction for a boss silhouette patrol. Simple readable shapes, low saturation gray-blue, beige-gray, muted green, and muted orange accents. Lightly funny but oppressive office atmosphere. No readable text, no real company logos, no realistic faces, no detailed interactive objects baked into the background.
```

负面提示词：photorealistic, realistic office photo, 3D render, cyberpunk neon, horror, high saturation, complex texture, detailed readable text, real logo, realistic human faces, anime detailed characters, cluttered foreground, tiny unreadable objects, overpainted illustration, cinematic realism, glossy UI, mobile game ad style.

```text
A warning-state variant of the same 16:9 side-view office cross-section background, in a muted Flash-style cartoon office look. Same composition and layout as the base office background.
```
